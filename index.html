For lights with a motion sensor, firmware 14 or later; flash colours and the top flash need 17. The top flash is timed from each light’s last throw, so it lands on balls and flat throws but not spun clubs, and it starts on a light’s second throw. The flash is each light’s own. Every throw by any light counts, and the mode changes on all the lights paired over the radio and connected here. Lights keep these settings when you disconnect.

Lights

Put lights in groups to control each group on its own. On firmware 22 or later the lights in a group also pair with each other over the radio, so next time connecting one of them is enough. Older lights pair by holding their buttons 5 s (10 s unpairs), and paired lights follow each other whatever their group.

Lights you aren’t connected to are listed too when a connected light hears them over the radio (firmware 12 or later on both).
